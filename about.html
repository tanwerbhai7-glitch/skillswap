Our story

Skills are currency too.

SkillSwap started as a corkboard in a co-working space — a place to pin an index card offering guitar lessons for web design help. We just built it a website.

Why we built this

Not everyone has spare cash for lessons, but almost everyone has something worth teaching. SkillSwap makes that trade visible: post what you know, find what you want to learn, and swap directly with another person — no fees, no middlemen.

We believe the best learning happens person-to-person, with real accountability on both sides. That's why every swap ends with a review, and why your reputation on the board is worth more than any subscription plan.

What we stand for

Fair exchange

Every trade should feel like an even swap — time and skill for time and skill.

Real accountability

Reviews and a visible history keep the community trustworthy.

Open to everyone

Whatever you know is worth teaching, and worth trading for something new.
